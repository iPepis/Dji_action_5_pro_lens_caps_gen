Printing tips
  • Print with the text face on the bed, open end up. No supports.
  • 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls. For two-colour text pause at 0.8 mm (before layer 5) and swap filament.
  • Measure your lens ring, then pick the fit. Too tight? Choose a larger size.
